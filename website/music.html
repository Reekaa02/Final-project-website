Top 10 Must-hear

1. Merdekarya

Short description: A legendary live music bar and venue in Petaling Jaya, Selangor. Iconic spot hosting live original bands, singer-songwriters, and indie acts nightly, with a diner bar for food/drinks and a dedicated live bar—serving cheap beers (from RM8), cocktails, tuak, langkau, plus simple eats in a no-frills, mature-audiences-only setting.

Why it's interesting: As Malaysia's original live music bar (est. 2013), it champions 100% local/independent artists with frequent gigs, spoken word, comedy, and a passionate crowd—perfect for authentic nightlife, discovering emerging talent, late-night hangs, and supporting the scene in a laid-back, affordable way. A true gem for music lovers seeking real vibes over commercial spots.

Location: Jalan 5/57, Section 5, Bukit Gasing, 46000 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia (near old town PJ, entrance on main road).

Socials: Instagram: @merdekarya • Facebook: merdekarya • Website: merdekarya

Time: Tue-Sat 6:00 PM–1:00 AM (Diner Bar); Thu-Sat 8:00 PM–1:00 AM (Live Bar); Closed Sun-Mon

Price: affordable RM8+, drinks, cocktails cheap, food reasonable

★★★★

2. SimplyLive

Short description: Energetic spot featuring live Mandopop hits by the resident band "Stars of Simply Live" (Wed-Sat), plus other genres, local talents, guest artistes, and a menu of food & drinks ( cocktails, hearty eats) in a sensational, music-focused setting.

Why it's interesting: Kuala Lumpur/PJ's go-to for Mandopop lovers, delivering thrilling live band energy, iconic Chinese pop tunes, heartfelt performances, and a lively nightlife atmosphere perfect for after work, singing along, chilling with friends, or enjoying quality live entertainment in a modern, well-designed space. A standout for authentic Mandopop vibes and memorable nights out.

Location: Lot No. 1.012, First Floor, The Square, Jaya One, Jln Profesor Diraja Ungku Aziz, Seksyen 13, 46200 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia.

Socials: Instagram: @tinboxgroup.my • Facebook: tinboxgroup.my • Website: SimplyLive

Time: Wed & Thu: 5:00 PM–12:00 AM; Fri & Sat: 5:00 PM–1:00 AM

Price: Rm 13-40 reasonable for live venue; drinks affordable, food portions satisfying and value-packed

★★★★
