Top 10 Must-Visit

1. Sekinchan

Short description: A scenic coastal rice-farming and fishing village in Sabak Bernam, Selangor, known for vast paddy fields and fresh seafood. Famous for golden-green paddy landscapes best March-May/Sept-Nov.

Why it's interesting: Offers peaceful countryside escapes with Instagrammable paddy views, cheap fresh seafood feasts, and simple rural charm perfect for day trips from KL to unwind, snap photos, or enjoy authentic Malaysian village life away from the city.

Location: Sekinchan, Sabak Bernam, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: -

Time: daytime, during sunset

Price: free

★★★★

2. Kuala Selangor (Fireflies & Bukit Melawati)

Short description: FNature-rich coastal town featuring Bukit Melawati hill with monkeys and panoramic views, plus the famous Kampung Kuantan Firefly Park with river boat tours showcasing one of the world’s largest firefly colonies.

Why it's interesting: Perfect blend of wildlife, culture, and romance. Ideal for family trips, eco-tourism lovers, and couples looking for a peaceful yet unique weekend escape near KL.

Location: Kuala Selangor, Selangor, Malaysia (about 1 hour from KL)

Socials: -

Time: best times to go Daily 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Price: RM20–50 | Firefly boat tours

★★★★

3. Batang Kali

Short description: A cool, hilly retreat in Hulu Selangor, known for hot springs, waterfalls, and outdoor vibes. Natural hot springs, Ulu Yam waterfalls, orchid farms, hiking trails, and local eateries in a refreshing setting.

Why it's interesting: Relaxing soaks, nature hikes, and fresh air great for unwinding or family picnics.

Location: Batang Kali, Hulu Selangor, Malaysia (about 1 hour from KL).a

Socials: -

Time: Open daily from 9AM - 10PM

Price: RM 3 - 60 affordable activities

★★★★

4. Gamuda Cove

Short description: A modern eco-resort and adventure hub in southern Selangor, blending nature with fun attractions.SplashMania waterpark, Paya Indah Discovery Wetlands, adventure parks, cycling trails, and eco-activities in a green township.

Why it's interesting: Thrills like slides/kayaking plus wildlife spotting—perfect for family fun or nature getaways.

Location: Gamuda Cove, Kuala Langat, Selangor, Malaysia (about 45 mins from KL).

Socials: -

Time: -

Price: RM 35 - 50 prices vary between activities

★★★★

5. Sungai Chiling Waterfall (Kuala Kubu Bharu)

Short description: A pristine forest river and waterfall area featuring a majestic three-tier waterfall, fish sanctuary, and short jungle hike with river crossings.

Why it's interesting: Refreshing natural pools and adventure-style hiking make it a favourite for nature lovers and day-trip explorers.

Location: Near Kuala Kubu Bharu, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: #SungaiChiling

Time: Daily 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Price: RM5–10 | Entry permit

★★★★

6. Pulau Carey

Short description: A small island in Selangor known for Mah Meri Orang Asli culture, mangrove environments, golf resort, and water park attractions.

Why it's interesting: Unique cultural immersion combined with eco-adventures and resort-style relaxation near Kuala Lumpur.

Location: Pulau Carey, Kuala Langat, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: #PulauCarey

Time: Daily 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Price: RM20–150 | Depends on activity or resort

★★★★

7. Bagan Lalang beach

Short description: A scenic coastal beach area in Sepang, Selangor, known for golden sands and seaside relaxation.

Why it's interesting: Clean sands for swimming/sunsets, family-friendly vibes, and fresh seafood—great for easy beach days close to KL.

Location: Bagan Lalang, Sepang, Selangor, Malaysia (about 1 hour from KL)

Socials: -

Time: Beaches open 24 hours

Price: RM10–200 | Varies by activity

★★★★

8. Taman Botani Negara Shah Alam

Short description: A large botanical park featuring forest trails, lakes, gardens, cycling paths, and picnic spots within a protected green reserve.

Why it's interesting: Perfect for nature lovers who want a peaceful escape without leaving the city. Ideal for jogging, cycling, picnics, and light hiking in a well-maintained environment.

Location: Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: #TamanBotaniNegara

Time: Daily 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Price: RM3–5 | Entry fee

★★★★

9. Morib Beach

Short description: A laid-back coastal beach known for calm shores, kite flying, picnics, and nearby historical landmarks like Jugra Hill.

Why it's interesting: Relaxed beach vibes without crowds, ideal for quiet seaside afternoons close to KL.

Location: Morib, Banting, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: #MoribBeach

Time: Daily 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Price: RM0 | Free entry

★★★★

10. Batu Caves

Short description: Iconic limestone cave complex and Hindu temple site featuring 272 colourful steps, cave shrines, and the giant Lord Murugan statue.

Why it's interesting: Combines spiritual significance, impressive architecture, and iconic photo spots — a must-visit cultural landmark.

Location: Batu Caves, Gombak, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: #BatuCaves

Time: Daily 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM

Price: RM0 | Free entry

★★★★
