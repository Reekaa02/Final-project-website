Top 10 Must-eats

1. Shell Out

Short description: Shell Out Kota Damansara is a popular seafood restaurant specializing in Cajun-style Louisiana boil seafood — think fresh crabs, prawns, clams, mussels, and more, tossed in addictive signature sauces (like their famous Shell Bang), served straight on the table (no plates needed!) for a fun, messy, hands-on dining experience.

Why it's interesting: if you're a seafood lover this place is for you! A hands-on, casual seafood boil experience where fresh seafood (crab, prawns, clams, mussels, crawfish, etc.) is tossed in flavorful sauces like the signature Shell Bang, Cajun, garlic butter or lemon pepper. You eat directly with your hands perfect for families , celebrations with your friends, or messy seafood feasts.

Location: 16-1, Jalan PJU 5/15, Dataran Sunway Damansara, Kota Damansara, 47810 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia (around Kota Damansara area)

Socials: Instagram • Facebook (search @villageparkofficial)

Time: Monday – Sunday: 11:30 AM – 10:00 PM (Delivery, Dine In, Pick-up)

Price: RM150–300+ per person depending on what you order

★★★★

2. Mios & Co

Short description: Mios & Co is a cozy bistro-style cafe serving brunch, lunch, and dinner, western comfort food like sandwiches, pasta , fish & chips, wagyu nasi goreng, croffles , cakes, and aesthetic specialty coffees and drinks.

Why it's interesting: Super popular as a work-from-cafe spot with chill, cozy vibes, free WiFi, great music, and Insta-worthy food & drinks perfect for casual hangouts, catch-ups with friends, or productive sessions. Known for hearty mains and creative drinks at pocket-friendly prices.

Location: 38, Jalan Rugbi 13/30, Seksyen 13, 40100 Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia (Shah Alam area).

Socials: Instagram: @miosandco_ • TikTok: @miosandco

Time: Open daily, Mon–Thu: 10:00 AM – 11:00 PM Fri–Sun: 9:00 AM – 11:30 PM

Price: RM20–50+,wagyu items higher but still seen as value-for-money.

★★★★

3. Strangers at forty7

Short description: Strangers at 47 is a popular, award-winning café in Petaling Jaya, Selangor, specializing in creative fusion crepe known for innovative crepes like Nasi Lemak Crepe, Korean Fried Chicken Crepe, Big Breakfast Crepe, and various dessert options.

Why it's interesting: It stands out for its unique Malaysian-Western fusion twists on crepes, consistently praised for yummy food, creative flavors, and a relaxed vibe perfect for brunch, meals, or hangouts. It's been a neighborhood favorite for years with multiple awards and strong local love.

Location: 45, 47, 49 Jalan 17/45, Seksyen 17, 46400 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia (in the PJ area).

Socials: Instagram: @strangersat47 • Facebook: strangersat47 • Website: strangersat47.my

Time: open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM

Price: around RM20–RM35

★★★★★

4. Arabica Restaurant

Short description: Arabica Restaurant a highly-rated restaurant in Petaling Jaya, Selangor, specializing in authentic Arabic and Middle Eastern cuisine, offering flavorful grilled meats like shish tawook, lamb kebab, chicken kebab, mix grills, shawarma, plus Syrian & Yemeni influences.

Why it's interesting: Stands out for juicy, perfectly grilled Arabic dishes, generous portions, affordable authentic flavors, and late night availability ideal for Middle Eastern food cravings, family meals, or late dinners in the Kota Damansara area. Praised for value, service, and consistent quality.

Location: G10, Cova Square, Jalan Teknologi, Kota Damansara, PJU 5, Taman Sains Selangor, 47810 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia.

Socials: Instagram: @arabicamy • Facebook: Arabicamy • Website: arabicarestaurant.my

Time: Daily from 10:00 AM to 1:45 AM or 2:00 AM

Price: affordable; mains like mix grills around RM70–RM80, individual items cheaper

★★★★★

5. Soooka

Short description: A cozy, Muslim-friendly café in Damansara Perdana, Petaling Jaya, Selangor, specializing in unique Asian-fusion homecooked-style dishes blending Malaysian, Teochew and Thai influences.

Why it's interesting: Features standout old fusion comfort food with creative local twists soulful braised duck noodles and specialty chewy cakoi from a 20+ year vendor make it a flavorful, nostalgic yet innovative spot in PJ.

Location: 11A-1, Jalan PJU 8/5A, Damansara Perdana, 47820 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia.

6. Tasty Chapathi

Short description: A popular chain restaurant in Petaling Jaya, Selangor, specializing in authentic Northern Indian cuisine with fresh chapathi and flavorful curries. serving classic North Indian dishes like tandoori chicken, butter chicken, paneer pasanda, chicken briyani, raj kachori, premium thali, lunch combos, plus desserts like kulfi all halal-certified, with generous portions and comforting flavors.

Why it's interesting: Known for bold, authentic yet approachable North Indian tastes (not too salty/spicy), fresh handmade chapathi, value-for-money sets, and a warm, family-friendly vibe—perfect for hearty meals, gatherings, or discovering reliable Indian eats in PJ. Locals praise the consistent quality, variety, and satisfying curries.

Location: PJ Area Petaling Jaya, Selangor, The Curve, Mutiara Damansara.

Socials: Instagram: @tastychapathirestaurant • Facebook: TastyChapathi • Website: tastychapathi

Time: Daily 11:00 AM–10:30/11:00 PM

Price: RM 20–40

★★★★

7. Nasi Ayam Hailam Damansara

Short description: A long-standing, halal-certified restaurant in Damansara Utama (Uptown), Petaling Jaya, Selangor, specializing in flavorful Hainanese style chicken rice. Why it's interesting: decades-old favorite for authentic yet halal Hailam chicken rice (Chinese-origin style perfected for all races), juicy tender chicken, aromatic rice, consistent quality, and great valu ideal for quick lunches, family meals, or cravings in the busy Uptown Damansara area.

Location: No. 102, Ground Floor, Jalan SS 21/39, Damansara Utama, 47
