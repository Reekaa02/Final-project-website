Top 10 Must-Shop

1. OKGO Store (SS15, Subang Jaya)

Short description: Curated streetwear and thrift fashion store featuring Y2K, vintage, and hype-inspired pieces. Clean racks, good curation, and trendy fits without feeling messy.

Why it's interesting: A favourite among students and streetwear fans. You’ll find unique tops, pants, and accessories that feel current but not overplayed.

Location: SS15 Subang Jaya, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @okgostore • Facebook: okgostore

Time: Daily: 1:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Price: RM25–120

★★★★

2. ReFash (ss15)

Short description: Secondhand fashion chain focusing on modern, wearable pieces from casual to office wear. Clean, organised, and easy to browse.

Why it's interesting: Great for people who want thrifting without digging chaos. Ideal for everyday outfits and wardrobe basics.

Location: 15, Jalan SS 15/4, SS 15, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor.

Socials: Instagram: @refashmy • Facebook: refashmy • Website: Refash

Time: Daily: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Price:RM10–79

★★★★

3. 2nd STREET Bandar Utama

Short description: Japanese-style thrift store offering branded secondhand clothing, bags, and accessories in excellent condition.

Why it's interesting: Quality-controlled thrift shopping with brands like Uniqlo, Nike, Coach, and more — very different from typical bundle stores.

Location: Unit G-1, Westpoint Home Gallery, Lebuh Bandar Utama, Bandar Utama, 47400 Petaling Jaya, Selangor

Socials: Instagram: @2ndstreetmalaysia • Facebook: 2ndstreetmalaysia • Website: 2ndstreet

Time: D10:00 AM - 10:00 PM

Price: RM30–300

★★★★★

4. Jalan Jalan Japan (Klang)

Short description: Massive Japanese thrift warehouse selling clothing, shoes, bags, and random fashion finds imported directly from Japan.

Why it's interesting: Treasure-hunt vibes. You never know what you’ll find — from vintage denim to quirky Japanese fashion.

Location: Klang Parade Area, 41050 Klang, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @jalanjalanjapan • Facebook: jalanjalanjapan • Website: Jalanjalanjapan

Time: Daily: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Price: RM5–150

★★★★

5. Shimokita Space

Short description: A minimalist creative space inspired by Tokyo’s Shimokitazawa, combining café vibes, curated fashion, lifestyle pop-ups, and community-driven events in a calm, aesthetic setting.

Why it's interesting: A hidden gem for creatives and slow-living lovers. Great for weekend chill sessions, browsing local brands, casual coffee meetups, or discovering pop-up markets and small exhibitions without the mall crowd.

Location: 94, Jalan SS 15/4b, Ss 15, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor

Socials: Instagram: @shimokitaspace

Time: Daily 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Price: RM10–50 | Café items and pop-up pricing vary

★★★★

6. JBR bundle (USJ 21)

Short description: Local-style bundle thrift shop with racks of oversized tees, jackets, denim, and sportswear.

Why it's interesting: A favourite among hardcore thrifters who enjoy digging for hidden gems. Known for frequent restocks and affordable prices, making it great for budget shoppers, resellers, or anyone experimenting with streetwear and vintage styles.

Location: USJ 21, 47630 Subang Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: #JBRBundle

Time: Daily: 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Price: RM5–80 | Budget-friendly bundle pricing

★★★

7. Spiral Store

Short description: Curated vintage clothing shop with a mix of streetwear, vintage, and standout fashion pieces displayed in an eclectic and creative space. Offers everything from everyday cool fits to bold statement items.

Why it's interesting: A hidden gem in Taman Paramount, Spiral Store blends vintage, Y2K, and modern street styles — perfect for fashionistas after unique finds. The vibe is artsy and immersive, making browsing fun.

Location: 3a, Jalan 20/14, Taman Paramount, 46300 Petaling Jaya, Selangor

Socials: Instagram: @spiralstore

Time: 7Thu–Sun: 2:00 PM – 9:00 PM (Closed Mon–Wed)

Price:RM25–150

★★★★★

8. Against Lab (Petaling Jaya)

Short description: Malaysian homegrown streetwear brand store known for bold graphics, oversized silhouettes, and contemporary urban fashion. Features statement tees, hoodies, caps, and seasonal drops in a clean, modern retail space.

Why it's interesting: A strong representation of local fashion culture with international streetwear energy. Popular among youth and creatives who want standout pieces that still feel wearable and expressive.

Location: 34, Lorong 1/77A, Changkat Thambi Dollah, Off, Jln Pudu, Kg. Dollah, 55100 Kuala Lumpur, Wilayah Persekutuan Kuala Lumpur

Socials: Instagram: @againstlab • Facebook: againstlab

Time: Daily 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM

Price: RM89–299 | Mid-range streetwear pricing

★★★★

9. Wanderlust + Co (Mid Valley Megamall)

Short description: Contemporary jewellery boutique offering minimalist, elegant designs made with gold vermeil, sterling silver, and semi-precious stones. Known for delicate layering pieces, everyday wear jewellery, and gift-ready collections.

Why it's interesting: A fashionable jewellery brand that blends modern femininity with timeless design. Perfect for daily wear, gifting, or stacking pieces that elevate simple outfits without being over-the-top.

Location: Mid Valley Megamall, Lingkaran Syed Putra, 59200 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @wanderlustandco • Facebook: wanderlustandco

Time: Daily 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Price: RM129–699 | Premium everyday jewellery pricing

★★★★

Short description: Contemporary fashion store offering stylish everyday wear for men and women — from basics to trendy casual pieces. Known for comfortable fabrics, clean cuts, and easy‑to‑style outfits suitable for both workday and weekend looks.

Why it's interesting: A go‑to stop for shoppers who want quality basics and modern casual fashion without paying premium brand prices. Great for building a versatile wardrobe with pieces that mix and match easily.

Socials: Instagram: @bashclothing.co • Facebook: bashclothing
