Top 10 Must-Do

1. Thrill at Sunway Lagoon

Short description: Malaysia's top multi-park theme park in Bandar Sunway, Petaling Jaya. 90+ attractions across water park, amusement rides, wildlife park, extreme park, scream park, and Nickelodeon zone.

Why it's interesting: All-in-one fun for families or adrenaline seekers—slides, wave pools, animal encounters, and shows.

Location: Bandar Sunway, Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @sunwaylagoon • Facebook: sunwaylagoon

Time: Daily 10:00 AM–6:00 PM

Price: RM150–RM200 | One-day pass

★★★★

2. Watch Fireflies

Short description: Magical mangrove boat tour in Kampung Kuantan. Evening boat ride through mangroves to see thousands of twinkling fireflies.

Why it's interesting: Romantic, unique natural light show—one of the world's largest firefly colonies.

Location: Kampung Kuantan, Kuala Selangor, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: -

Time: Tours 7:30 PM–10:00 PM

Price: RM20–RM50 per person (depending on sides)

★★★★

3. Explore Zoo Negara (National Zoo)

Short description: Malaysia's premier zoo in Ulu Klang. 10-acre park with 5,000+ animals, including pandas, tigers, giraffes, and a mini safari.

Why it's interesting: Great for families—animal encounters, shows, and conservation focus.

Location: Ulu Klang, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @zoonegaramalaysia • Facebook: zoonegaramalaysia

Time: Daily 9:00 AM–5:00 PM

Price: RM50–RM80

★★★★

4. Walk on Sky Mirror (Kuala Selangor)

Short description: Natural mirror-like beach during low tide. Vast reflective salt flats for stunning mirror photos with sky and clouds.

Why it's interesting: Surreal, Instagrammable views—best at sunrise or sunset.

Location: Kuala Selangor (tours from jetty), Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: -

Time: Low tide days only

Price: RM75–RM100 with tour

★★★★

5. Adventure at i-City

Short description: Digital lights and theme park in Shah Alam. LED-lit wonderland with City of Digital Lights, SnoWalk, WaterWorld, FunWorld, and rides.

Why it's interesting: Colorful night vibes, snow room, water fun—great for evening hangs.

Location: Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @icitymalaysia

Time: Daily 5:00 PM–late

Price: Varies by zone

★★★

6. Hike Bukit Tabur

Short description: Panoramic hill trail in Hulu Langat. Moderate hike with quartz ridge views over lakes and forests.

Why it's interesting: Stunning sunrise/sunset views and nature immersion—popular for photos.

Location: Hulu Langat, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: -

Time: Daytime recommended

Price: RM5 per person

★★★★

7. Shop & Dine at Sunway Pyramid

Short description: Egyptian-themed mega mall in Bandar Sunway. 1,000+ stores, fashion brands, ice rink, cinema, and dining in pyramid structure.

Why it's interesting: Fun themed shopping with endless options—great for a full day.

Location: Bandar Sunway, Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @sunwaypyramid • Facebook: sunwaypyramid

Time: Daily 10:00 AM–10:00 PM

Price: RM50–RM85 per person

★★★★

8. Splash at SplashMania Waterpark

Short description: Modern water park in Sepang. High-speed slides, wave pool, lazy river, and family zones.

Why it's interesting: Thrilling water fun in a clean, modern setting ideal for hot days.

Location: Gamuda Cove, Sepang, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: -

Time: Daily 10:00 AM–6:00 PM

Price: RM50–RM100

★★★★

9. Relax at Farm in the City

Short description: Interactive farm in Seri Kembangan. Animal feeding, pony rides, fruit picking, and nature walks.

Why it's interesting: Hands-on farm fun for kids and families—educational and relaxing.

Location: Seri Kembangan, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @farmincity

Time: Daily 9:00 AM–6:00 PM

Price: RM50–RM60

★★★★

10. Skytrex Adventure Shah Alam

Short description: Outdoor adventure park with treetop obstacle courses, zip lines, rope bridges, and climbing challenges suitable for all ages.

Why it's interesting: Perfect for adrenaline seekers and nature lovers. Combines physical activity with scenic forest views, great for group outings or family adventures.

Location: Bukit Cerakah, Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @skytrexmalaysia • Facebook: skytrexmalaysia

Time: Daily 9:00 AM–6:00 PM

Price: RM70–RM120 per person

★★★★
