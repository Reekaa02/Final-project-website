Top 10 Must-Try

1. Shah Alam Art Gallery

Short description: Gallery space at Laman Budaya featuring contemporary art exhibitions, local artists, and rotating displays in a relaxed lakeside setting. Exhibitions often showcase Malaysian creativity and provide immersive visual art experiences.

Why it's interesting: A great place to explore local art, attend exhibitions and creative workshops, and discover emerging artistic talent in Selangor.

Location: Laman Budaya, Persiaran Tasik, Section 14, 40000 Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials:• Website: Galeri Shah Alam

Time: Typical gallery hours ~10:00 AM – 5:00 PM (check current listings)

Price: Free – moderate (varies by exhibition)

★★★★

2. Sasaran Art Park Kuala Selangor

Short description: Large outdoor art park & creative festival space blending murals, sculptures and community art installations set within green surroundings.

Why it's interesting: One of Selangor’s most ambitious public art projects — perfect for photography, walking tours and art appreciation outdoors.

Location: Jalan Sasaran, Sungai Buloh, 45800 Jeram, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @sasaranartpark • Facebook: Sasaran Art Park • Website: Sasaran Art Park

Time: 9:30 AM – 12:30 PM (check for events)

Price: Free – donation optional

★★★★★

3. G13 Gallery

Short description: Contemporary art space in Petaling Jaya showcasing modern Malaysian artists across painting, mixed media and experimental works.

Why it's interesting: Great for discovering fresh voices in the Malaysian art scene and attending openings or artist talks.

Location: Kelana Square, Jalan SS 7/26, 47301 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @g13gallery • Facebook: G13 Gallery • Website: G13 Gallery

Time: ~11:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Price: Free entry

★★★★

4. Artfordable Gallery

Short description: Friendly art gallery focusing on approachable art pieces — from abstract to floral — at accessible prices.

Why it's interesting: Perfect for first‑time art buyers or casual visitors wanting to own Malaysian artwork without breaking the bank.

Location: Jalan SS21/60, Damansara Utama, 47400 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @artfordablemgallery • Facebook: Artfordable Gallery – Malaysia • Website: Artfordable

Time: Daily

Price: Free

★★★★

5. Awegallery

Short description: Contemporary art gallery showcasing local and regional artists with a rotating programme of exhibitions, workshops, and art events.

Why it's interesting: Offers a community‑centric vibe and creative programming that often includes art fairs and meet‑the‑artist sessions.

Location: Jalan 20/13, Taman Paramount, 46300 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @awegallery • Facebook: Awegallery

Time: 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Price: Free

★★★★

6. Core Design Gallery

Short description: Subang Jaya’s contemporary art hub focused on curated exhibitions and emerging artists.

Why it's interesting: An intimate gallery with thought‑provoking exhibitions and opportunities to meet curators and artists.

Location: Jalan SS 15/2A, SS 15, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @corecontemporaryart • Facebook: Core Design Gallery

Time: 12:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Price: Free

★★★★

7. ZHAN Art | Space

Short description: Creative gallery space at Jaya One featuring visual arts and cultural programming.

Why it's interesting: Hidden gem for contemporary art lovers with dynamic exhibitions and photo‑friendly installations.

Location: Jaya One, Jalan Profesor Diraja Ungku Aziz, 46200 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @zhanartspace • Facebook: ZHAN Art | Space • Website: ZhanArt Space

Time: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Price: Free

★★★★★

8. Shah Alam theatre

Short description: Traditional performing arts theatre hosting plays, dance, drama productions and community cultural shows.

Why it's interesting: A cultural landmark in Shah Alam where local theatre troupes and productions showcase storytelling through stage art.

Location: Jalan Persidangan, Seksyen 14, 40000 Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Facebook: Majlis Teater Negeri Selangor • Website: Shah Alam Theatre

Time: Varies by show

Price: RM10–RM80 (typical)

★★★★

9. Petaling Jaya Performing Arts Centre

Short description: Intimate performance venue for theatre, dance, spoken word, and stage arts including community showcases.

Why it's interesting: Great for catching local plays, improv and theatre workshops — a creative hub for performing arts outside big auditoriums.

Location: Second Floor, 1 Utama E, Bandar Utama, 47800 Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: @pjpac_official • Facebook: Petaling Jaya Performing Arts Centre

Time: Varies by schedule

Price: RM15–RM70

★★★★

10. Laman Seni Street Art – Shah Alam

Short description: Public outdoor mural art district featuring creative murals, colourful graffiti, and Instagram‑worthy walls scattered around Seksyen 2 & Seksyen 7.

Why it's interesting: Free, large‑scale urban art that’s great for photos, exploring local artistic expression, and leisurely wanderings.

Location: Seksyen 2 & Seksyen 7, Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia

Socials: Instagram: #lamansenishahalam

Time: Public access (24 hrs)

Price: Free

★★★★
